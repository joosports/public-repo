PAGE 01 — PLAYER INTRODUCTION

Player Overview

Tse Long Tin is a 14-year-old Hong Kong U15 representative and a regular starting player for Kitchee Sports Club U16, primarily playing as a centre back and also as a defensive midfielder.

Already competing above his age group, Long Tin recorded 14 appearances for Kitchee U16 during the 2025–26 season, alongside 20 appearances at U14 level. He previously served as vice-captain of Kitchee U14 and was selected for the Hong Kong Football Association (HKFA) Talent Identification Programme.

Standing 186 cm tall, he also has a recorded 100 m personal best of 11.80 seconds and a 200 m best of 24.00 seconds.

His international youth representative experience, exposure to older age groups, leadership background and athletic profile provide a foundation for his continued football development.

Development Background

  • Regular starting experience for both Hong Kong U15 and Kitchee U16.
  • Playing above his age group at club level.
  • Former vice-captain of Kitchee Sports Club U14.
  • Selected for the HKFA Talent Identification Programme (2012 age group).
  • Athletic background including 100 m and 200 m sprint competition experience.
